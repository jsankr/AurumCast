Data, method and limits

Live sources (fetched by your browser)

    AurumCast runs entirely in your browser. No data is stored on any server; settings and the prediction ledger live in this browser's local storage. Not investment advice. Futures trading on MCX carries leverage risk.

    Settings

    Default is 15% (10% BCD + 5% AIDC), effective 13 May 2026. Change it here if the duty changes again.

    MCX futures trade at a premium or discount to import parity (carry, local demand, contract month). Enter the live MCX price from your broker and AurumCast stores the gap and applies it to every ₹ figure. Leave blank for pure parity.
